What is Internet Banking?

COMPUTER PROJECT

Internet banking is a service that allows customers to access certain bank services through an official website or mobile application using the internet.

01
Convenience

Check account details and pay bills without visiting a branch.

02
Speed

Many digital transactions can be processed quickly.

03
Security

Banks use security controls such as encryption and authentication.

04
Availability

Many services are available online at any time, subject to maintenance and bank rules.

🛡️
Banking safety rules
Use official apps/websites, strong unique passwords and trusted networks. Never share your OTP, PIN or password. Check the payee details before approving any real payment.
